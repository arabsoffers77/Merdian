About Us

Designing Better Lives

At Meridian Engineering Consultancy, we shape the built environment to enhance quality of life and respond to the evolving needs of cities and communities. Based in Oman, our practice brings architecture, engineering and planning together, combining a global outlook with a deep understanding of place.

We approach each project as an integrated system, aligning human needs with the demands of site, performance and resources. Through research, analysis and design, we establish a clear rationale for every decision and a practical path from concept to delivery.

We believe design quality is expressed in how a place is experienced, how effectively it performs and how well it endures. We unite ideas and ambition with engineering precision, assessing our decisions by the value they create for people, communities and the environment—today and throughout the life of each project.

Services

Comprehensive consultancy, end to end.

All services
01

Urban Planning

We develop urban plans that integrate land use, mobility and infrastructure, creating connected places that respond to community needs and guide future growth.

02

Port & Harbour Engineering

We plan and design port facilities and marine structures around navigational requirements, operational needs and site conditions, supporting efficient operations, structural safety and future expansion.

03

Coastal Studies & Engineering

We analyse waves, currents, sediment transport and shoreline change to develop coastal protection solutions that work with natural processes and balance development needs with the marine environment.

04

Infrastructure Engineering

We design integrated road, water, drainage and utility networks, prioritising reliable performance, efficient operation and maintenance, and capacity for future growth.

05

Residential & Tourism Development

We design residential communities and tourism destinations that combine a strong sense of place with functional efficiency, drawing on each site’s character to enrich everyday living and the guest experience.

06

Project Management

We manage project scope, programme, cost and risk, coordinating stakeholders to support informed decisions, maintain control of performance and achieve project objectives from planning through handover.

07

Environmental Studies

We assess environmental impacts and site sensitivities, developing mitigation measures and management and monitoring plans to inform design decisions and support environmental compliance.

08

Construction Supervision

We oversee site works to verify compliance with approved designs and specifications, monitor quality, progress and safety requirements, and address technical matters through to handover.
