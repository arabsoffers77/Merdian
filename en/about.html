About Us

Designing Better Lives

At Meridian Engineering Consultancy, we believe design carries a responsibility to people and the future of places. We bring together architectural vision and engineering expertise, grounding each project’s ambition in a thorough understanding of its context and the practical demands of delivery. Through collaboration across disciplines, we develop solutions that balance human experience, technical performance and responsible resource use—creating places that enrich lives today and respond to the needs of generations to come.

About Us

Integrated Expertise. A Vision Beyond Borders.

Meridian Engineering Consultancy is a multidisciplinary consultancy registered in the Sultanate of Oman. We combine local knowledge with a global perspective to develop solutions that respond to each project’s ambitions and requirements.

We see professional partnerships as a platform for exchanging knowledge and advancing design and engineering. We collaborate with specialist firms across Europe, the United States, Asia and Africa, bringing diverse expertise and complementary disciplines together around a shared vision for each project.

We provide our clients with a single point of accountability, leading project teams and coordinating disciplines to deliver cohesive solutions and aligned decisions from concept through execution.
