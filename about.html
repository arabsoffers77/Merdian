Who We Are

A multi-disciplinary consulting firm, registered in Oman.

Meridian Engineering Consultancy (MEC) is a multi-disciplinary consulting firm registered in Oman. In its journey to success, MEC forms associations with other specialized foreign firms from Europe, USA, Asia & Africa as necessary, to ensure providing outstanding engineering consultancy professional services.

That model lets us scale specialist expertise around each commission — the client deals with one accountable firm, while the delivery team always matches the problem at hand.
